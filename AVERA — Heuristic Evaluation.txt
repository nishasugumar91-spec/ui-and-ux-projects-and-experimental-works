AVERA — Heuristic Evaluation


1. Introduction

Heuristic evaluation is a usability inspection method used to identify potential usability problems in a user interface by evaluating it against established usability principles.
The AVERA women's clothing e-commerce website is designed to provide a clean, elegant, and user-friendly shopping experience for young, fashion-conscious women, particularly college students and working professionals. The evaluation focuses on navigation, product discovery, product information, shopping cart, checkout, payment, and account management.


2. Evaluation based on Nielsen’s 10 Usability Heuristics


1. Visibility of System Status

AVERA should clearly communicate the current state of user actions, such as adding products to the bag, applying filters, placing orders, and tracking deliveries.
Recommendation:
Display confirmation messages, loading indicators, order status labels, and estimated delivery dates.



2. Match Between the System and the Real World

The interface should use familiar shopping terms such as Categories, New Arrivals, Wishlist, Shopping Bag, and My Orders.
Recommendation:
Use simple language, recognizable icons, familiar currency formatting, and delivery dates that users can easily understand.



3. User Control and Freedom

Users should be able to change their selections, remove unwanted items, adjust quantities, and return to previous steps without losing their progress.
Recommendation:
Provide clear Back, Remove, Edit, Reset Filters, and Continue Shopping actions.



4. Consistency and Standards

AVERA should maintain consistent colors, typography, button styles, icons, spacing, and navigation throughout all screens.
Recommendation:
Use the same mauve primary buttons, beige backgrounds, pista-green accents, and familiar shopping icons across the website.




5. Error Prevention

The design should help users avoid mistakes, particularly when selecting clothing sizes, entering delivery information, or placing orders.
Recommendation:
Validate address and payment fields, require a size selection when necessary, and show an order summary before the final purchase.



6. Recognition Rather Than Recall

Users should not need to remember product details, prices, sizes, or previous selections while shopping.
Recommendation:
Display product images, selected sizes, saved wishlist items, recently viewed products, and cart contents where relevant.



7. Flexibility and Efficiency of Use

AVERA should support both first-time shoppers and frequent customers by making product discovery and purchasing efficient.
Recommendation:
Provide search, sorting, category navigation, reusable saved addresses, wishlist access, and quick add-to-bag actions.



8. Aesthetic and Minimalist Design

The interface should look premium without overwhelming users with excessive text, decorations, or competing elements.
Recommendation:
Maintain the mauve, beige, and pista palette, use adequate whitespace, prioritize product photography, and keep promotional content secondary to shopping tasks.



9. Help Users Recognize, Diagnose, and Recover from Errors

When an error occurs, users should understand what went wrong and how to fix it.
Recommendation:
Provide clear messages for invalid addresses, unsuccessful payments, unavailable sizes, and out-of-stock products, along with actionable recovery options.



10. Help and Documentation

Users should have access to useful guidance when they need assistance with sizing, payments, delivery, returns, or refunds.
Recommendation:
Include size guides, fit descriptions, FAQs, return policies, delivery information, and accessible customer support.
